What I work on

Most of my work sits where a business meets its software: the website a customer finds, the system that answers them, and the internal tools that keep orders, recruits or workloads moving. I design the architecture, write the code, connect the integrations and stay with the product after launch.

Right now that means four ventures. At Ascent Vision, which I co-founded with Grayson Sorrentino, we build websites, custom software and AI systems for businesses. KnockFiber recruits door-to-door fiber sales reps, crews and agencies for partner sales organizations. OrbitRoute models where compute workloads should run across terrestrial cloud and potential orbital capacity. At Practically Natty I’m CTO & managing partner, responsible for the store’s software and operations tooling. Each one, with its status, is on the work page.

How I work

Ship live, then prove it. Public products have links you can inspect. When an audit found a pricing constant on OrbitRoute with the wrong sign, the fix wasn’t softer wording — it was correcting the number and publishing a comparison that recommends the competitor.

Make the result measurable. Define what success means before building. Keep source timestamps, clear ownership and useful reporting close to the workflow so the team can judge what improved.

Honest instruments. A forecast tool that can’t beat a coin flip should say so. Earnings claims stay out of recruiting feeds. That discipline is enforced by guard tests in CI, not by good intentions.
