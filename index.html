Method

How he works

Ship live, then prove it. Every claim across this network maps to a URL you can open. When an audit found a pricing constant on OrbitRoute with the wrong sign, the fix wasn't softer wording — it was correcting the number and publishing a comparison that recommends the competitor.

Automate the operator, not just the task. The systems above run themselves: scheduled jobs, watch folders, self-checking ledgers, health sweeps that report red before a customer does.

Honest instruments. A forecast tool that can't beat a coin flip should tell you it can't beat a coin flip. Earnings claims stay out of recruiting feeds. That discipline is enforced by guard tests in CI, not by good intentions.
